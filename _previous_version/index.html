The review loop

Four steps, with a person in charge of every decision.

Store the rules once

An admin adds guidelines, preferred terms, approved facts with owners and expiry dates, and a register of claims that need evidence.

Paste a draft

Posts, emails, web copy or ad text. Exact checks run live as you type.

Resolve each finding

Every flag shows why, cites the rule, and offers a rewrite as a before and after. You accept, edit or dispute with a reason.

Get it approved

Reviewers see what is still open, read your disputes, comment, and approve. Every version and decision is on record.

Built to be trusted

A checker people ignore is worse than none. These choices keep the findings worth reading.

No source, no flag

A finding appears only if it points to a real rule or approved fact. When the optional Claude check suggests something it cannot back with a rule and an exact quote, the finding is dropped and the count is shown.

Facts expire

Every approved fact has an owner and a valid-to date. A price that was right in September gets flagged in November, and the owner is named.

Fix the guideline, not only the post

When creators keep disputing a rule, Insights marks that rule as unclear and shows what they said, so the owner can rewrite it.

Made for everyone who touches a draft
